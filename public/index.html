FAQ

  • How much does a project cost?

    We work at a fixed price. After a first call we scope the project and send a quote, so you know the cost before any work starts.

  • How long does it take?

    Most projects reach a first usable release in 4–8 weeks. Larger products are split into milestones, each with its own release.

  • Do you work with clients outside Romania?

    Yes. We work remotely with businesses across the EU, in English or Romanian.

  • Can you build for both iOS and Android?

    Yes. We build mobile apps in Flutter, so one codebase ships to both the App Store and Google Play.

  • Do you build desktop apps?

    Yes. We build desktop apps in .NET, and they can share a backend with your web and mobile apps.

  • What do we get at the end?

    The source code, documentation and deployment setup, so your team can take it from there or we can keep building it together.

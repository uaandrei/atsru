FAQ

  • Is a prototype a working app?

    No. It's a clickable design: it looks and behaves like your app, but there's no code or backend behind it yet. That makes it fast and cheap to change.

  • How long does it take?

    Usually 2–4 weeks, depending on how many screens and flows the idea needs.

  • What happens after the prototype?

    When the idea holds up, we build the real product with you as a web, mobile or desktop app.

  • How much does it cost?

    Prototypes are fixed price. After a first call we send a quote, so you know the cost before we start.
